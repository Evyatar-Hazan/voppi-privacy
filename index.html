Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026
Developer: Evyatar Hazan
Contact: 7127547@gmail.com

Voppi is an expressive AI companion for Android phones and Wear OS. The phone works without a watch; the watch uses a paired phone for AI connectivity. This policy explains how both apps handle information.

Information processed

Voice and conversation data. During Gemini Live conversations on the phone or watch, microphone audio is sent to Google's Gemini API; watch audio is relayed through the paired phone. For OpenAI conversations, Android's speech-recognition service converts audio to text and the prompt is sent to OpenAI. Prompts, transcripts and generated responses are processed by the selected provider. Optional phone captions remain in activity memory and clear when the activity closes. They are separate from encrypted conversation context and explicitly approved memories. Voppi does not operate a developer-owned server or send this content to the developer.

AI provider credentials. The user supplies a personal Gemini or OpenAI API key and may choose a model identifier. Voppi stores credentials in encrypted local storage on the Android phone and uses them only to authenticate requests to the selected provider. Credentials are not sent to the watch or the developer.

Local profile. The user's chosen display name and the chosen assistant name are stored in encrypted local storage on the phone. They are included in the selected provider's instructions only to personalize responses. They are not sent to the developer.

Motion sensors. Voppi reads the watch accelerometer locally to detect an extreme shake and trigger a character reaction. It does not request heart-rate, body-sensor, or activity-recognition permission. Accelerometer readings are not sent to the developer or used for advertising or profiling.

Offline interactions. When Voppi shows OFFLINE, touch reactions, short character lines, expressions, and haptics are selected and presented locally on the watch. They are not sent to an AI provider or the developer and are not stored as conversation history.

Character packs. The selected character-pack identifier is stored in encrypted local storage on the phone and synchronized only to the paired watch through Wear OS Data Layer. It changes response style, face color, and local reactions; it does not add personal data or new network recipients.

Calendar, reminders, contacts, calls, and Spotify. With permission, Voppi reads relevant calendar event title, time, and location and may include them in a request to the selected AI provider when the user asks about their schedule. Event descriptions and attendees are not read. Optional reminder settings stay on the phone. After a separate opt-in and an explicit request to create an event, the selected provider may process the requested title, time, duration, and location to prepare a local notification. Tapping it opens the Calendar app's visible confirmation screen; Voppi never saves the event silently. Contact names and phone numbers are searched locally. After a separate opt-in in Features and a request to call, up to three matching contact names may be sent to Gemini to select and confirm the intended contact. This opt-in is off by default from Voppi 0.4.26 and can be turned off in Features. Phone numbers stay on the phone and are not sent to an AI provider. Only the app can place a call after a separate explicit spoken confirmation; a model-generated tool argument cannot authorize dialing. Music requests are passed to the installed Spotify app without access to Spotify credentials.

Local memories. Voppi may store a small amount of conversation context and user-approved memories in encrypted local storage on the phone. Users can delete all of this local assistant data from Voppi after an explicit confirmation. Separately stored provider credentials and the local profile are not part of this action.

Sharing, retention, and security

Provider retention. Local deletion does not erase records held by an AI provider. Google may use unpaid Gemini inputs and outputs to improve its products and for human review; do not send personal, sensitive or confidential information to unpaid services. Use an eligible paid service for personal-data features. OpenAI API abuse-monitoring logs may retain content for up to 30 days by default. From Voppi 0.4.24, OpenAI requests disable optional response storage; this does not disable provider safety logs or delete prior records. See Gemini API terms and OpenAI data controls for the rules applicable to your account.

Voice, transcript, prompt, calendar fields requested by the user, opted-in matching contact names, local-profile names, and generated-response data are shared only with the AI provider selected by the user to provide the requested functionality. Provider handling is governed by the terms and privacy documentation applicable to the user's Gemini or OpenAI API access. Voppi does not sell personal data, show advertising, or include third-party analytics SDKs.

The developer does not retain voice, sensor, profile, or conversation data on a developer-owned server. Conversation context and user-approved memories can be deleted together from Voppi, and deletion requests can also be sent to the contact address below. Removing the apps also removes their app-local data, subject to Android operating-system behavior. Voppi uses Android platform encryption for credentials, the local profile, and local memory, and HTTPS for provider requests.

Data deletion

Open Settings in Voppi on the phone, tap “Delete local conversation data,” review the confirmation, and tap “Delete.” This removes encrypted conversation context and user-approved memories. Provider keys and the local profile are retained. “Delete selected provider key” removes the selected credential separately. Voppi has no developer-owned server copy of voice, sensor, profile, conversation or memory data. Users may also email 7127547@gmail.com with a Voppi data-deletion request.

Age eligibility and changes

Voppi and its AI-powered functionality are intended only for users aged 18 or older. This policy may be updated when functionality or legal requirements change.

מידע שמעובד

  • חיפוש שמות ומספרי טלפון נעשה מקומית בטלפון. לאחר אישור נפרד במסך היכולות ובקשת חיוג, שמות של עד שלוש התאמות עשויים להישלח ל־Gemini לבחירת איש הקשר ולאישורו. החל מ־Voppi 0.4.26 האפשרות כבויה כברירת מחדל וניתן לכבות אותה במסך היכולות. מספרי הטלפון נשארים בטלפון ואינם נשלחים לספק AI. רק האפליקציה יכולה לחייג לאחר אישור קולי מפורש נוסף; ארגומנט שנוצר בידי המודל אינו יכול לאשר חיוג. בקשת מוזיקה מועברת לאפליקציית Spotify המותקנת ללא גישה לפרטי ההתחברות.

Google עשויה להשתמש בקלט ובפלט של Gemini ללא תשלום לשיפור מוצריה ולבדיקה אנושית; אין לשלוח לשירותים ללא תשלום מידע אישי, רגיש או סודי. ליכולות שמשתמשות במידע אישי יש להשתמש בשירות מתאים בתשלום. OpenAI עשויה לשמור תוכן בלוגי בטיחות עד 30 ימים כברירת מחדל. החל מ־Voppi 0.4.24 הבקשות מבטלות שמירת תגובות אופציונלית ב־OpenAI; אין בכך ביטול לוגי בטיחות או מחיקת רשומות קודמות. התנאים העדכניים נמצאים ב־תנאי Gemini API וב־בקרות הנתונים של OpenAI.
